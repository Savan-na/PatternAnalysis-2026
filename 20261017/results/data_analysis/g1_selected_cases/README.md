# G1 selected raw MRI overlays

These six four-plane figures pair unprocessed Train MRI slices with their
ground-truth contours. They were chosen to inspect unusual shape, intensity,
background share, small Prostate size, and two Bladder component flags. The
displayed planes show no obvious whole-image/mask shift; they do not establish
pixel-level accuracy or assess every slice. The Bladder flags have separate
close views in [G1 Bladder review](../g1_bladder_review/README.md).
