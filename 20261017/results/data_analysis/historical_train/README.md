# Historical Train statistics

These six SVG figures and their CSV/JSON source tables preserve an earlier
Train-only analysis of 143 scans from 26 patients. They show rare Rectum and
Prostate voxels, uneven repeat-scan counts, organ-volume variation, and an old
resampling comparison. They motivate current data checks but do not describe
the restarted preprocessing or patch-sampling pipeline. The longer
[historical mixed-source review](../../reports/HipMRI_Pretraining_Data_Analysis.md)
is archived separately; current G1 findings are in the formal research report.

The two `.bak` files are preserved versions of that earlier review.
